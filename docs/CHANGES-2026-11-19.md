## 1. Defect fixed: pro-rata share amounts (calculation, priority A)

**Before.** Four overlapping checks (§3, §3b, §3c, §3d in `audit-logic.ts`) each added an amount for the *same* share difference. Each priced it as `statement total × (billed% − expected%)`. That "total" is often the tenant's own charge, which makes the result meaningless.

**Example.** Fictional fixture: the statement bills 10.00%; the lease says 8.08%.

- Headline: $5,086 ($1,272 + $1,270 + $1,272 + $1,272).
- The statement's own arithmetic gives $661,437.20 × 1.92 / 100 = $12,699.59.

**After** (`src/services/share-math.ts`, section 3 of `audit-logic.ts`):

**Expected share.**
- The share the lease states is used first. A square-footage ratio is used only if the lease states no share.
- If the stated share and the square-footage ratio disagree by more than 0.5 points, no expected share is assumed.

**Dollar amount.**
- One finding and one amount at most.
- The amount is calculated only when the statement's figures show the pool the share was applied to (pool × billed% = tenant charge, within $1 or 0.05%).
- The finding shows the inputs, the formula, the exact result and the rounding.
- If the pool is not shown, the finding is a high-severity question with no amount, and the letter asks how the share was determined.

**Underbilled share.** This is a low note with no amount. It is never presented as savings and never raised in the letter.

**Stored audits** are contained when they are read. Rows are not rewritten. `src/lib/legacy-containment.ts`, "share class":

- Old share findings are identified by category plus the exact sentence the old engine generated.
- The amount is withheld. The stored percentages and the evidence are kept.
- Duplicates collapse into one item.
- The headline is reconciled by the existing rule.
- A share-specific notice is shown (`legacyShareContained` in `quantification.ts`).

`src/services/__tests__/fixtures/legacy-share-audit.json` was produced by running the *pre-fix* engine on fictional fixtures.

**Business follow-up.** Paid audits stored before this change may have shown inflated or wrongly based share amounts. The read-time containment corrects what owners see after deploy. Whether to contact affected customers is a business decision. To size it, run this **read-only** query on production (review it before running):

```sql
select count(*) from audits
where status = 'completed'
  and (select count(*) from jsonb_array_elements(coalesce(free_findings,'[]'::jsonb) || coalesce(paid_findings,'[]'::jsonb)) f
       where f->>'category' in ('Pro-Rata Share Mismatch','CAM Allocation Discrepancy','Tenant Allocation Discrepancy')
         and (f->>'potential_savings')::numeric > 0) >= 1;
```

## 2. New: CAM cap and base-year calculator (public page `/cam-cap-calculator`)

`src/lib/cap-calculator.ts` and `src/components/cam-cap-calculator.tsx`.

**Cap methods:**
- Non-cumulative, measured against the prior year's allowed amount or its actual amount.
- Cumulative, compounding.
- Cumulative, non-compounding.

**Inputs and results.**
- The tenant enters only the expenses their lease caps. Nothing is classified by expense name.
- Years must be consecutive; gaps are refused, not interpolated.
- Results show every formula and the same figures under each method.
- The page explicitly does not decide the method, the covered expenses, amendments or gross-up.

**Base year and expense stop:** total amount or per square foot.

**Known answer** (fictional): 5% cap, $100,000 base. The total above the cap is $5,292.50, $3,850, $1,750 or $2,000 depending on the method.

Linked from `/cam-calculator` and the cap guide, and listed in the sitemap.

The cap guide's "How LeaseGuard helps" text no longer says the audit calculates how far a cap is exceeded (it does not). Two unsourced phrases were softened: "errors are common" and "thousands of dollars in overcharges".

## 3. New: audit-rights clause and supporting-document request (paid letter page)

**Audit-rights clause** (`src/lib/audit-rights.ts`):
- Audit-right and dispute-window sentences are quoted from the lease at analysis time, with page, period and trigger.
- Number words, parenthesised digits and months are read. Business days are recognised but never converted into a date.
- A date is calculated only from a start date the customer enters, with a caution that it is not a legal deadline. There is no countdown.
- The dispute letter is unchanged. It still contains no deadlines.

**Document request** (`src/services/document-request.ts`):
- An editable request for records, built from the CAM Review Plan's items and per-item document lists. It includes no amounts, deadlines or statutes.
- It quotes an audit clause only when one was found, and never claims that clause entitles the tenant to copies.

**Access:**
- Both are behind the existing owner-plus-verified-payment gate (`/api/audit/[id]/letter`).
- The quoted clause is removed from the unpaid `/api/audit/[id]` response (`toPublicAudit`).
- Audits created before this change have no clause record. The page says so instead of implying the lease has none.
